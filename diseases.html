Lumpy Skin Disease
CRITICAL

Symptoms

  • Fever, loss of appetite, reduced milk production
  • Skin nodules (2-5cm) across body
  • Swollen lymph nodes, lameness
  • Eye/nasal discharge, salivation
  • Emaciation in severe cases

Treatment & Control

  • Isolation of infected animals immediately
  • LSR vaccine (Nepal strain) administration
  • Supportive therapy: antibiotics, anti-inflammatories
  • Tick control and vector management
  • Slaughter of severely affected animals
🐮
Foot and Mouth Disease
HIGH

Symptoms

  • Blisters on mouth, tongue, feet
  • Excessive salivation ("slobbering")
  • Lameness, reluctance to move
  • Fever (104-106°F), reduced milk yield
  • Heart issues in young animals

Treatment & Control

  • FMD trivalent vaccine every 6 months
  • Strict movement quarantine
  • Foot baths with 2-5% CuSO4
  • Soft bedding, pain management
  • Disinfection of premises
🥛
Brucellosis
MEDIUM

Symptoms

  • Abortion in last 3rd of pregnancy
  • Stillbirths, weak calves
  • Reduced milk production
  • Infertility, irregular estrus
  • No clinical signs in males usually

Treatment & Control

  • RB51/S19 vaccination in calves
  • Test and slaughter/cull policy
  • Hygiene in calving areas
  • Milk pasteurization mandatory
  • Bull testing and replacement
🐐
Peste des Petits Ruminants (PPR)
MEDIUM

Symptoms

  • High fever (104-105°F), depression
  • Mucopurulent nasal/ocular discharge
  • Erosive stomatitis, diarrhea
  • Pneumonia, bronchopneumonia
  • Mortality 30-90% in naïve herds

Treatment & Control

  • Sungri96 vaccine annually
  • Strict biosecurity measures
  • Supportive therapy + antibiotics
  • Quarantine new introductions 30 days
  • India PPR-free target: 2026
🐖
African Swine Fever
CRITICAL

Symptoms

  • High fever (106°F), anorexia
  • Red skin blotching, hemorrhages
  • Diarrhea (bloody), vomiting
  • Labored breathing, abortions
  • Acute mortality up to 100%

Treatment & Control

  • No vaccine available in India
  • Total depopulation of infected farms
  • 24-month standstill period
  • Swill feeding ban enforced
  • Wild boar surveillance critical
🐔
Avian Influenza (Bird Flu)
MEDIUM

Symptoms

  • Sudden drop in egg production
  • Swelling of head/eyes/combs
  • Twisted neck (torticollis)
  • Bloody diarrhea, respiratory distress
  • High mortality in layers (H5N1)

Treatment & Control

  • Culling within 3km radius
  • Disinfection + litter disposal
  • Vaccination (H5N1) in high-risk areas
  • Wild bird surveillance program
  • Live bird market regulations
☠️
Anthrax
LOW

Symptoms

  • Sudden death (bloody froth)
  • High fever, staggering gait
  • Bloating without rigor mortis
  • Dark "tar-like" blood from orifices
  • Splenic enlargement (3x normal)

Treatment & Control

  • Sterne vaccine annually
  • Penicillin immediately on suspicion
  • Do NOT open carcass (spore formation)
  • Deep burial + lime treatment
  • Soil pH monitoring (alkaline risk)
🐃
Haemorrhagic Septicaemia (HS)
HIGH (Feb 2026)

Symptoms

  • Sudden high fever (104-106°F)
  • Severe respiratory distress
  • Throat swelling, salivation
  • Subcutaneous edema (neck, brisket)
  • Mortality 70-80% untreated

Treatment & Control

  • Oil-adjuvant HS vaccine annually
  • Immediate antibiotics (Oxytetracycline)
  • Anti-inflammatory drugs
  • Monsoon season vaccination critical
  • Water buffalo most susceptible
🐄
Bovine Viral Diarrhea (BVD)
MEDIUM

Symptoms

  • Diarrhea (may be bloody)
  • Fever, depression, nasal discharge
  • Reduced milk production
  • Persistent infections (PI calves)
  • Reproductive losses

Treatment & Control

  • BVD vaccine (killed/modified live)
  • Ear notch testing for PI animals
  • Cull persistently infected calves
  • Closed herd policy recommended
  • Semens testing mandatory
🐥
Infectious Bursal Disease (IBD)
HIGH

Symptoms

  • Watery white diarrhea
  • Ruffled feathers, depression
  • Tremors, sternal bursitis
  • Immunosuppression (7-21 days)
  • Mortality 20-60% in broilers

Treatment & Control

  • Intermediate/variant vaccines D-78
  • Broiler vaccination day 14
  • All-in-all-out farming
  • Litter management critical
  • Antibiotics for secondary infections
🐓
Newcastle Disease (Ranikhet)
CRITICAL

Symptoms

  • Respiratory distress, gasping
  • Greenish diarrhea
  • Torticollis (twisted neck)
  • Swollen head (velogenic form)
  • Nearly 100% mortality (VVND)

Treatment & Control

  • LaSota/R2B vaccine schedule
  • Day 1, 7, 21 vaccination program
  • Strict biosecurity - wild birds
  • Reportable disease (OIE List A)
  • Complete farm depopulation VVND
🦇
Rabies
CRITICAL

Symptoms

  • Excessive salivation ("mad dog")
  • Paralysis, bellowing
  • Hydrophobia, aggression
  • Self-mutilation
  • 100% fatal once clinical

Treatment & Control

  • Pre-exposure vaccination dogs
  • Post-exposure prophylaxis humans
  • Animal vaccination campaigns
  • Stray dog population control
  • Immediate reporting mandatory
🐄
Johne's Disease (JD)
MEDIUM

Symptoms

  • Chronic weight loss despite appetite
  • Progressive diarrhea (cow-pat type)
  • Emaciation, bottle-jaw edema
  • Reduced milk yield (30-50%)
  • 2-4 year incubation period

Treatment & Control

  • No effective treatment
  • Indigenous JD vaccine (4+ yrs)
  • Calving hygiene paramount
  • Manure management critical
  • ELISA screening + culling
🦟
Tropical Theileriosis
HIGH

Symptoms

  • High fever (105-107°F)
  • Anaemia, jaundice
  • Swollen lymph nodes
  • Laboured breathing, weakness
  • Exotic/crossbred most susceptible

Treatment & Control

  • Buparvaquone (2.5mg/kg IV)
  • Tick control (Deltamethrin)
  • Rakshavac/RakshaTDAR vaccine
  • Crossbred calf vaccination
  • Weekly tick inspection
