✅ Field Checklists

Use these quick checklists alongside the official PDFs when responding to an outbreak or vaccination drive.

Outbreak Visit – Minimum Kit

  • PPE: gloves, mask, coverall, boots, sanitizer.
  • Sampling: vacutainers, swabs, transport media, labels, ice box.
  • Documentation: case forms, GPS‑enabled phone, consent where needed.

Vaccination Drive Basics

  • Verify cold chain and expiry of vaccine vials before use.
  • Maintain animal register with tag, owner, dose and batch number.
  • Report daily coverage to district office or upload via EpiVet reports page.

Using External Portals

  • NADRES for national disease maps and risk forecasts.
  • FAO / OIE for updated global disease cards and manuals.
  • State AH websites for latest circulars and local SOP updates.
